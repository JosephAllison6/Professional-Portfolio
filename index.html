Exhaust silencers

Silencer designs

Designed a stacked insert model and an auger silencer model in SolidWorks for the Liberty University Formula SAE team.

Stacked insert

Selected as a top choice because its segmented geometry maximizes sound wave disruption, significantly reducing decibel output for competition noise compliance.

Auger silencer

Engineered to push a continuous, spiraling flow through the muffler and directly into the glass-packed core.

Manufacturability

Optimized the auger design using disks, keeping the spiraling flow while making it easy to manufacture.

Exhaust transient simulation

Static pressure contour in the 4-to-1 collector (ANSYS Fluent).

Custom UDF implementation

Programmed a C-based User-Defined Function to model time-dependent exhaust valve pulses and mass flow rates based on engine crank angle.

Transient flow analysis

Ran dynamic CFD simulations in ANSYS Fluent to visualize pressure wave propagation and identify high-restriction zones within the 4-to-1 collector.

Backpressure validation

Validated an optimized, equal-length runner design with highly balanced flow, limiting max backpressure variance to 0.06 PSI across all four headers.

Manifold optimization

Used static pressure contour data to refine the manifold geometry, supporting smooth exhaust scavenging and reduced pumping losses.
